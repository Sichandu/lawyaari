About LegalDost

आपका क़ानूनी दोस्त — Your legal friend, always available.

LegalDost was built with one simple belief: legal knowledge should not be a privilege. In India, millions of MSMEs, workers, tenants, and common citizens face legal problems every day — but cannot afford a lawyer for basic questions.

Our Mission

To make legal awareness accessible to every Indian in their own language — Hindi, Telugu, and Hinglish — at zero or minimal cost.

What We Do

  • Free legal Q&A in Hindi, Telugu, and Hinglish
  • AI-generated legal notices for cheque bounce, MSME payment recovery
  • Complaint drafts for consumer, police, medical, and labour issues
  • Legal reply generation for notices from banks, landlords, and employers
  • Priority answers for urgent legal questions

Who Built This?

LegalDost is built by an independent founder from Andhra Pradesh, India — passionate about making justice accessible. This is a bootstrapped MVP built to solve a real problem faced by millions of Indians who don't know their rights.

Important Disclaimer

LegalDost is an AI-powered legal information service. We are not a law firm and do not provide formal legal advice. All outputs are AI-generated and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before use in legal proceedings.
