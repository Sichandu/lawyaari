Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LegalDost.

General

Is LegalDost a law firm?

No. LegalDost is an AI-powered legal information platform. We are not a registered law firm and do not provide formal legal advice. Always consult a qualified lawyer before taking legal action.

What languages does LegalDost support?

Hindi (हिंदी), Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hinglish (Hindi in English script). We're working on adding more regional languages.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We do not share your personal information with third parties. Your phone number is used only for login and usage tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Free Tier

How many free chats do I get?

Hindi and Hinglish users get 5 free chats per day. Telugu users get 3 free chats per day. The counter resets every 24 hours automatically.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start chatting immediately without login. Login is required only for premium services and to save your chat history.

Premium Services

Are the AI-generated legal documents legally valid?

The documents follow proper Indian legal formats and cite correct laws. However, they are AI-generated and should be reviewed by a lawyer before official use. They serve as a strong starting point that saves you time and lawyer fees for drafting.

We accept all payment methods via Razorpay — UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets.

Technical
