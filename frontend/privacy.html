Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026. Your privacy matters to us.

LegalDost collects minimal data necessary to provide our service. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Information We Collect

  • Mobile number: Used for OTP-based login only.
  • Name & Gender: Provided voluntarily during registration for personalization.
  • Chat messages: Stored to improve AI accuracy and for admin analytics.
  • Payment information: Processed by Razorpay. We do not store card details.
  • Device info: IP address and user agent for security and rate limiting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our legal assistance service
  • To process payments via Razorpay
  • To send OTP for login verification
  • To track usage limits (free tier enforcement)
  • To analyze aggregate usage patterns (no individual targeting)

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure MongoDB Atlas servers. Chat history and OTP records are retained for service improvement. Payment records are retained for compliance purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay: For payment processing
  • Groq / OpenAI: Your chat messages are sent to AI providers for response generation. Do not include highly sensitive personal information in chats.

5. Your Rights

  • Right to access your data: WhatsApp us with your mobile number
  • Right to delete your data: WhatsApp us to request account deletion
  • Right to opt out of analytics: Contact us

6. Cookies

We use localStorage (browser storage) to save your session token and preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

7. Children's Privacy

LegalDost is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
